If you’ve received a tempting new job offer but still love your current role, a well‑crafted counter‑offer letter can turn the tables in your favor. It’s more than a polite “I’d like to stay”; it’s a strategic document that outlines the compensation, responsibilities, and career growth you need to stay motivated. Get the wording right, and you’ll give your employer a clear, professional reason to match—or even exceed—what the market is offering you.

## 1. When Is a Counter Offer Worth Pursuing?

A counter offer isn’t a default reflex; it should be a calculated move. Ask yourself these three questions:

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------------|
| **Do I have a clear career path here?** | If your manager can outline promotion timelines or new project ownership, the offer becomes more than a salary bump. |
| **Is the new compensation realistic for my role?** | Use the **[Salary Estimator](/tools/salary-estimator)** to benchmark the market rate for your title, location, and experience. |
| **Will I stay engaged after the negotiation?** | If you’re already disengaged, a raise may only delay the inevitable departure. |

**Actionable tip:** Create a two‑column pros‑cons list. In the “Stay” column, list concrete benefits (e.g., mentorship, flexible schedule). In the “Leave” column, note what the new role offers (e.g., higher base, remote‑first culture). If the “Stay” side can be bolstered with a solid counter offer, you have a stronger negotiating position.

## 2. Core Elements Every Counter Offer Letter Must Contain

A counter‑offer letter is essentially a formal request that mirrors a job offer but from your current employer’s perspective. Include these five sections:

1. **Header & Contact Info** – Use the same format as a business letter; include your name, title, and contact details at the top.
2. **Opening Statement** – Reference the external offer and reaffirm your commitment to the company.
3. **Specific Requests** – Break down the compensation package you’re seeking: base salary, bonus, equity, benefits, and any non‑monetary items (remote days, leadership training, etc.).
4. **Justification** – Cite measurable achievements, market data, and future contributions that justify the request.
5. **Closing & Call to Action** – Suggest a meeting timeframe and thank the reader for their consideration.

**Example snippet:**  
> “Based on my recent project that increased quarterly revenue by 18%, and after reviewing comparable roles on the market via the Salary Estimator, I am requesting an adjusted base salary of $95,000, a 10% performance bonus, and the option to work remotely two days per week.”

## 3. Step‑by‑Step Template + Real Counter Offer Letter Example

Below is a ready‑to‑use template. Replace bracketed text with your details.

```
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
[Company Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Date]

[Manager’s Name]
[Manager’s Title]
[Company Name]

Dear [Manager’s First Name],

I appreciate the recent conversation about my career development and the external offer I have received from [Competing Company]. I remain deeply committed to the mission of [Current Company] and would like to explore a revised compensation package that reflects my contributions and market value.

Specifically, I am requesting:
- Base salary: $[Desired Salary] (an increase of $[Amount] over my current salary)
- Performance bonus: [Percentage] of annual salary, tied to measurable goals
- Equity: [Number] of RSUs vesting over [Timeframe]
- Remote work: [Number] days per week
- Professional development: Sponsorship for the [Specific Course/Certification]

Over the past [Timeframe], I have:
- Led the [Project Name] that delivered a $[Dollar Amount] cost saving.
- Increased client retention by [Percentage] through the new onboarding process.
- Mentored three junior analysts, two of whom were promoted.

These outcomes align with the company’s strategic objectives and demonstrate my capacity to drive further growth. I would welcome a meeting next week to discuss how we can structure this package and outline next steps.

Thank you for considering my request. I look forward to continuing to contribute to [Company Name]’s success.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
```
